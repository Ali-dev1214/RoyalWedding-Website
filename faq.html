How do I book Royal Wedding Planner?＋

Start by submitting an inquiry with your preferred date, events and package. Our team can then discuss the details with you.

Can I customize a package?＋

Yes. Packages are starting points. You can discuss services and arrangements that fit your celebration.

How far in advance should I book?＋

Earlier is generally better, especially for popular wedding dates. Contact us to discuss your preferred schedule.

Do you manage multiple events?＋

Yes, the planning concept supports multiple events on the same day, subject to the final schedule and resources.

What payment methods are available?＋

Payment arrangements can be discussed during consultation. This demo website does not process payments.

Can I change my booking details?＋

Changes can be discussed with the planning team and depend on availability and the agreed arrangements.

MORE PLANNING DETAILS

A few more helpful answers.

Some details are easiest to settle before you confirm your event plan.

Can I book only one event, such as Mehndi or Walima?＋

Yes. You can plan a single event or combine several events. Select the relevant event on the booking page to see a separate estimate for each one.

Are per-head prices the final confirmed cost?＋

No. The per-head figures shown here are sample UI rates. Final costs depend on the confirmed menu, venue, date, guest count and agreed inclusions.

Can I choose a different hall for each event?＋

Yes. The booking estimate allows a separate hall choice for Mehndi, Baraat, Walima and Mayun.

Can guest numbers change after I submit the inquiry?＋

Guest numbers can be discussed during planning. Any change may affect catering, seating and the final estimate.

Do packages include every listed service automatically?＋

Packages are illustrative starting points. Review their inclusions carefully and confirm the final list of services before making a decision.

Can I request a custom wedding theme?＋

Yes. Share colour ideas, inspiration images, cultural preferences and any special requirements in your inquiry.

Does this website save booking details or accept payment?＋

No. This version is a frontend-only demonstration. Forms show sample feedback and calculate estimates in the browser; they do not store information or process payments.

What should I prepare before the first consultation?＋

It helps to have your possible dates, event names, approximate guest counts, preferred city, budget range and a few visual ideas ready.
