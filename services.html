WHAT THE SERVICE CAN INCLUDE

Support for the big picture
and the smallest details.

Every celebration is different. These service areas can be selected individually or brought together as a complete planning experience.

01 · PLANNING

Wedding planning & coordination

Event schedule, planning checklist, supplier coordination, venue communication and on-the-day event flow.

  • Planning milestones
  • Event-day run sheet
  • Vendor coordination notes
02 · DESIGN

Décor, stage & styling

A visual direction for the stage, entrance, tables, floral elements, lighting and photo-friendly spaces.

  • Theme and colour direction
  • Stage and table styling
  • Lighting and floral concepts
03 · HOSPITALITY

Catering & guest experience

Meal planning coordination, service timing, guest seating flow and hospitality details.

  • Menu discussion
  • Dining and seating flow
  • Guest welcome arrangements

Photography & film

Coverage planning for key moments, family portraits, event highlights and a considered shot list.

  • Coverage schedule
  • Family photo coordination
  • Highlight moments list

Shortlisting venue styles and coordinating details with the suppliers selected for your event.

  • Hall style and capacity discussion
  • Supplier coordination
  • Setup and access timings
06 · PERSONAL TOUCHES

Couple & family arrangements

Getting-ready schedules, family entry moments, special announcements and personal requests.

  • Getting-ready schedule
  • Family moment planning
  • Special request checklist
